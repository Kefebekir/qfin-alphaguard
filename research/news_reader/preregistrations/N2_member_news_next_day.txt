PRE-REGISTRATION N2
Written 2026-10-16 13:43:43 UTC; approved by Efe before any result of this test was computed.

Does a member's news, read by a language model that knows nothing after the year before,
tell its next day against the other members with news?

HYPOTHESIS
Among S&P 500 members with news since the last close, those whose headlines lead a model,
trained only on earlier years, to forecast a better next day earn more from today's close to
tomorrow's close than those it forecasts worst. Mechanism: prices underreact to firm news
and absorb it over days, more for bad news (Tetlock 2008; Ke, Kelly and Xiu 2019;
Lopez-Lira and Tang 2023). Our measure (research/frontier/news_reaction.py): moves after
news carry on, moves without news reverse.

DATA
- Articles: as N1, tagging at most two stocks; an article counts for each tagged stock that
  is an S&P 500 member that day (the sp500 flag of data/raw/prices.parquet).
- A day's window for a member: from 16:00 New York time on the session before to 15:30 on
  the day, so that a market-on-close order can be sent before 15:50.
- Returns: the stored daily closes (adjusted for splits and dividends), from the day's close
  to the next session's close, less the equal-weighted mean of all members with news that day.

FEATURES (fixed here)
- As N1: ChronoBERT vintage Y-1 for test year Y; a member-day's features are the mean
  embedding of its window's headlines and log(1 + their number).

MODEL (trained by Efe; the protocol fixed here)
- Target: the next day's return less the mean of members with news, as above.
- Family: as N1 (principal components, ridge); at most 10 variants on development, each in
  the ledger.
- Policy: each day, equal weights long the top tenth of forecasts and short the bottom tenth
  among members with news (at least 20 such members, else no trade), bought or sold at the
  day's close and closed at the next close. Costs: 10 bps a round trip on each side.

SPLITS
- Development: test years 2018-2021, fitted on the years from 2016 before each.
- Holdout: 2022 to 9 October 2026, each year fitted on all years before it, run once after
  this file and the chosen variant are committed.

TEST
- The long-short's mean daily return after costs over the holdout: one-sided t >= 2.24
  (Bonferroni over N1 and N2).
- Reported, not part of the pass: before costs; long side and short side apart; each year;
  the same with log(count) alone.
- If it passes, the model is frozen on a card and judged forward in the shadow record; if it
  fails, it is recorded as failed.
