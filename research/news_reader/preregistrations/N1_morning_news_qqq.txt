PRE-REGISTRATION N1
Written 2026-10-16 13:43:43 UTC; approved by Efe before any result of this test was computed.

Does the morning's news, read by a language model that knows nothing after the year before,
tell the QQQ rule's day?

HYPOTHESIS
On days whose news before 10:00 New York time leads a model, trained only on earlier years,
to forecast a better day for the noise-area rule, the rule earns more. Mechanism: intraday
momentum is stronger when information arrives (Gao, Han, Li and Zhou 2018; Li, Sakkas and
Urquhart 2022); days with news trend, quiet days chop, and the rule earns on trends.

DATA
- Articles: every Benzinga article on Alpaca's news API (research/news_reader/news_all.py).
  An article is known at created_at, or at updated_at when that is more than 5 minutes later
  (the text kept may be the edited one; 2.5% of articles).
- A day's window: from 16:00 New York time on the session before to 09:55 on the day.
- The rule's day: momentum.py's NoiseAreaMomentum in the engine, guard_qqq.yaml with
  max_order_usd lifted, a 0.3 bps half spread, from 30,000 USD; its daily return from
  SessionClose values (backtest/shadow.daily_returns), every session, traded or not.

FEATURES (fixed here)
- For test year Y, ChronoBERT vintage Y-1 (manelalab/chrono-bert-v1-<Y-1>1231): each
  headline's embedding is the mean of the last hidden layer over its tokens (at most 64),
  computed once. Every year's features for that test year come from the same vintage.
- A day's features: the mean embedding of its window's headlines (768 numbers) and
  log(1 + the number of articles in the window).

MODEL (trained by Efe; the protocol fixed here)
- Target: the rule's day return divided by QQQ's 14-day volatility known at the open.
- Family: principal components of the day's mean embedding, fitted on the training years,
  and a ridge regression on them and the log count. Efe chooses the number of components
  and the ridge penalty on the development years only, from at most 10 variants, each
  recorded in the ledger with its development result.
- Policy: the rule's size that day times 1.5 if the forecast is in the top third of the
  training years' forecasts, 0.5 if in the bottom third, 1 otherwise. Nothing else changes.

SPLITS
- Development: test years 2018-2021, each fitted on the years from 2016 before it. Efe may
  look at these as often as he likes.
- Holdout: 2022 to 9 October 2026, each year fitted on all years before it with the variant
  chosen on development, run once after this file and the chosen variant are committed.

TEST
- The daily difference between the sized rule at the rule's risk (the scale from the
  training years) and the rule, over the holdout: one-sided t >= 2.24 (Bonferroni over N1
  and N2 at 2.5% each side).
- Reported, not part of the pass: the same with log(count) alone; each holdout year.
- If it passes, the model is frozen on a challenger card and judged forward in the shadow
  record (decision 0020). If it fails, it is recorded as failed.
