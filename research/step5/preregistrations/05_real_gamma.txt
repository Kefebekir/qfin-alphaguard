Written 2026-10-15T16:14:02Z, before any options open interest was downloaded or seen.

Question: does the options dealers' gamma, measured from real open interest, tell the
QQQ noise-area rule's good days from its bad ones? (Baltussen, Da, Lammers and Martens 2021:
intraday momentum on negative-gamma days, none on positive; Dim, Eraker and Vilkov 2024:
positive dealer gamma strengthens reversals, negative strengthens momentum.)

Data: OPRA open interest (Databento statistics schema; published about 06:30 ET for the
previous session's close, so known before the open) and contract definitions, for the QQQ
options and, as a second test, the SPY options; 2016-01-26 to 2026-10-09, every day the rule
has.

Measure, fixed now (the "naive" dealer gamma of the practitioner literature, SqueezeMetrics):
dealers are assumed long the calls and short the puts customers hold.
  GEX(day) = sum over calls of gamma x OI x 100 x S^2 x 0.01  -  the same sum over puts,
with S the underlying's last close, gamma from Black-Scholes with one volatility for every
contract (the VXN close for QQQ, the VIX close for SPY, from the session before), the 3-month
T-bill rate, no dividend, and every expiry from the next session to one year. GEX is divided
by the underlying's average daily dollar volume over the 20 sessions before, and ranked
against its own previous 250 sessions (no look-ahead).

Primary test (G1), on QQQ's GEX: the regression of the rule's daily result after costs on
that rank has a negative slope (more dealer gamma, worse for momentum). One-sided, passes if
t <= -2.0 over 2017-2026 (the rank needs a year). Reported beside it: the rule's Sharpe ratio
in each third of the rank, and each year.
Secondary (G2): the same with SPY's GEX. Both together: Bonferroni, t <= -2.24.

Neither GEX nor any options data has been looked at for this project before this note.

Amendment, written 2026-10-15T16:47:25Z, still before any open interest was looked at
(only one day, 2024-06-03, was downloaded to see the record layout; its values were not used):
Databento charges about 5 USD for QQQ's 2016-2022 statistics and 380 USD for 2016-2026,
because its records from 2023 are far larger. The free credit covers 2016-2022 only, so G1
and G2 are tested on 2017-2022 (the rank needs 2016). 2023-2026 is tested only if Efe pays
for it, with the same measure. The contract terms come from each record's OCC symbol, not from
the definition schema. Everything else is unchanged.

Implementation details, written 2026-10-15T17:07:05Z, before GEX was computed:
- Day t's GEX uses the open interest record published at 09:31 ET on day t (the close of the
  session before), known before the rule's first decision at 10:00.
- Contracts alive on day t count: expiry on or after day t and within 365 days. Time to expiry
  is the calendar days from day t to the expiry, at least half a day (0.5/365), so an option
  expiring on day t has a finite gamma.
- S, sigma (VXN or VIX close, /100) and the T-bill rate are those of the session before day t.
- The average daily dollar volume: the sum over minutes of close x volume, over the 20
  sessions before day t, from the stored minutes.
