PRE-REGISTRATION N6
Written 2026-10-22 18:55 UTC, from Efe's choices on the decisions page (1A 2A 3A 4A 5A 6A);
approved by Efe at 19:27 UTC, before any development run of N6.

Can a network we train ourselves read the news better than N4's principal components and
ridge? PIT-4B stays frozen as the eyes; everything after it is learned: a projection of each
headline, attention over a member-day's headlines, a small network on its context, and a head
joining them. Efe trains it.

WHAT IS ALREADY KNOWN
Every year of the data, 2016 to 9 October 2026, has been seen through N2's, N3's and N4's
holdouts: N4's chosen variant made +6.53 bps a day after costs on 2022-2026 (t +1.86), resting
on 2026 (+35.55 a day, +0.92 over 2022-2025). Before N6 was designed it was also seen that
PIT-4B's checkpoints drift and two of them have one dominant dimension (eyes_check.py), and
that in 2026 the S&P 500 members moved with SPY far less than in any year before
(n6_context.py). So no run on these years is an independent test of N6: its test is forward,
on sessions not yet seen, as N5's.

DATA (built and checked before this file: results_n6_eyes.txt, results_n6_context.txt,
results_n6_data.txt)
- The member-days, articles, windows (16:00 on the session before to 15:30), members and next
  returns of N3 and N4: 260,186 member-days, 2016 to 9 October 2026.
- The eyes: each headline's last-token state, read by PIT-4B's checkpoint of the year before
  its own, brought to 202412's scaled reading by method B (align_check.py, chosen by a rule
  fixed before its run), frozen in data/models/n6-eyes.npz, SHA-256
  bc95f2ed84130e156496728ae56a56a283243c25074cb8fa9e40e3ef85f2093d.
- A member-day's last 32 headlines by the time they became known, each with log(1 + its age
  in hours at 15:30), whether it counts from an edit, and whether it tags two stocks; and
  log(1 + its number of headlines).
- Its context, every number known by the close of the session before: the member's returns
  over 1, 5, 20 and 60 sessions and a year's momentum, volatility over 20 and 60, range, gap,
  traded value and turnover, distance from its 52-week high, beta and correlation with SPY,
  the sector fund it moves most alike and that fund's returns; SPY's returns and volatility,
  VIX, VIX9D, VIX3M and VVIX, the members' dispersion and co-movement; the weekday and the
  scheduled FOMC, CPI and payrolls days. The member's numbers scaled within each day, the
  market's on the training days only (n6_harness.py).
- The target, N4's: the next return less the day's mean over the member-days with news, over
  the member's 20-session volatility.

MODEL (n6_model.py; trained by Efe, the protocol fixed here)
- A learned projection of each headline's 4,096 numbers, attention over the member-day's
  headlines, a network on the context, a head on the pooled news, the count and the context;
  a second head on the size of the move. At most 250,000 trainable parameters (decision 6A).
- The loss is a lever: MSE on the target, or minus the long-short Sharpe ratio over a batch's
  days (decision 3A). So are the projection's size and start (principal components or random),
  the layers' sizes, dropout, the auxiliary weight, the learning rate, weight decay, epochs and
  days a batch. Nothing else is changed without an amendment.
- A variant is one set of levers; its forecast is the mean of five models, seeds 0 to 4.

DEVELOPMENT (decisions 1A and 2A)
- Years 2018, 2019, 2020 and 2021, each validated with models trained on every member-day from
  2016 that ends five sessions before the year's first.
- At most 10 variants, each recorded in the ledger before the next is run (n6_run.py).
- Each variant's two placebos are run beside it, with the same levers and seeds: its
  headlines and counts dealt again among the day's member-days, each member's context and
  return staying with it; and the context alone, without any headline or count.
- THE PLACEBO GATE (decision 4A): a variant can be chosen only if its mean a day after costs
  over the four years is above both its placebos'.
- THE CHOICE: of the variants through the gate, the one with the highest mean a day after
  costs over the four years, whatever it is. If none passes the gate, N6 fails here: no second
  look and no card.

THE SECOND LOOK (seen years; not a test)
- The chosen variant, run once after it is committed in variants/N6_chosen.json, on each year
  2022 to 9 October 2026, fitted on every member-day from 2016 that ends five sessions before
  the year's first.
- If its mean a day after costs is not above 0, N6 fails here: no card (decision 5A).
- Reported, not part of any pass: each year, before costs, the long and the short side apart,
  N6 less N4's variant 2 day by day with its t, and the placebos.

THE FORWARD TEST (decision 5A)
- The chosen variant fitted once, five seeds, on every member-day from 2016 to 9 October 2026,
  frozen on a challenger card with its weights' SHA-256; never fitted again.
- Each session from the card's first day: the headlines captured at 15:31 (qfin news-capture),
  counted by N2's rule, read by PIT-4B-202412 and scaled as n6-eyes.npz scales 202412; the
  context from data dated before the session, computed after its close; N4's variant 2's
  portfolio: a tenth a side, equal weights, ties in, at least 20 members, 2 bps a day. A
  session without a capture has no score and is listed.
- The test: decision 0020's betting test of each day's score against no trade, scale 1,
  alpha 0.00625 (wealth 160), half of what N5 left of the platform's 2.5%; the clip the
  largest absolute daily score of the second look, rounded up to 0.1%. The null hypothesis:
  every day, given what came before, the clipped score is expected to be at most 0; then the
  chance of a promotion is at most 0.625%, however often it is read.
- The first day is the first session after the card is committed. A promotion is evidence for
  Efe's decision record, never a switch. Reviewed on 31 December 2028; Efe may retire the card
  earlier, and its share stays spent.
- Reported each evening, not part of the test: as N5's, and N6 less N5's frozen N4, day by
  day, on the sessions both scored.

HARDWARE
The record runs the network in floating point. An FPGA version (int8, Arty A7) is built and
checked against it apart; it never changes the record.
