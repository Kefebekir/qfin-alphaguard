PRE-REGISTRATION N4
Written 2026-10-21 13:05 UTC; approved by Efe before any result of this test was computed.
Written after N2's and N3's holdouts were run (21 October 2026): see WHAT IS ALREADY KNOWN.

Can N3 be read with less noise? N3's chosen variant made +4.24 bps a day after costs on its
holdout, t +1.18, with a daily standard deviation of about 124 bps: a Sharpe ratio near 0.5,
which would need about 21 years of days to reach t 2.50. N4 is N3 with four ways to cut the
noise, chosen on the development years only.

WHAT IS ALREADY KNOWN
N4 is designed knowing N2's and N3's results on 2022 to 9 October 2026, among them that N3
lost 11.34 bps a day in 2025. So N4's run on those years is a second look at seen years, not
an independent test: its evidence is weaker than N3's was, and a pass earns a place in the
shadow record, judged forward on days not yet seen, never a trade. The news tests' 2.5% was
spent by N1, N2 and N3; N4 has its own one-sided 0.625% (t >= 2.50), Efe's choice.

DATA
As N3: the same member-days, windows (16:00 on the session before to 15:30), members,
returns (the day's close to the next session's close), at least 20 members with news for a
day to trade, and costs of 1 bp a round trip on each side, 2 bps a day.

FEATURES
N3's: the member-day's mean PIT-4B embedding (base-last or base-mean, each headline read by the
checkpoint of the year before its own) and log(1 + the number of headlines).

THE FOUR LEVERS (each variant sets each one; N3 is all four at their first value)
1. Target: "plain", N3's (the next return less the mean of that day's members with news), or
   "scaled", that divided by the member's volatility.
2. Side: "tenth", "fifth" or "third" of the day's members long, the same short. Members tied at
   an edge are all in (amendment 5).
3. Weights within a side: "equal", or "inverse" to the member's volatility, normalised so that
   each side sums to one.
4. Sectors: "none", or "neutral": each sector's members ranked apart, its top and bottom
   fraction taken (as many from each end, its members // 10, // 5 or // 3; a sector with too
   few members is left out that day), each sector holding on each side a weight in
   proportion to the members it gives.
A member's volatility: the standard deviation of its daily close-to-close returns over the 20
sessions ending the session before the day (known before the day's trades); the day's median
for a member with fewer than 20.
Sectors, statistical, one set a calendar year: the S&P 500 members on the year's first session
with at least 200 daily returns in the 252 sessions before it, grouped into 11 by Ward's
hierarchical clustering on the distance sqrt(2 (1 - correlation)) of those returns; a member
without the history, or new in the year, is in a twelfth group of its own kind. Nothing after
the year's first session is used.

MODEL (trained by Efe; the protocol fixed here)
- Family as N2 and N3: principal components and ridge (efe_model.make), components and penalty
  chosen with the levers. At most 10 variants on development in all, each in the ledger
  before the next is run.
- The variant with the highest mean a day after costs on the development years goes to the
  run on 2022-2026, whatever it is.

SPLITS
- Development: test years 2018-2021, fitted on the years from 2016 before each.
- The second look: 2022 to 9 October 2026, each year fitted on all years before it, run once
  after this file and the chosen variant are committed.

TEST
- The long-short's mean daily return after costs over 2022 to 9 October 2026: one-sided
  t >= 2.50.
- Reported, not part of the pass: before costs; the long side and the short side apart; each
  year; N4 less N3, day by day, with its t.
- If it passes, the model is frozen on a card and judged forward in the shadow record; if it
  fails, it is recorded as failed. Either way nothing trades on this run.
