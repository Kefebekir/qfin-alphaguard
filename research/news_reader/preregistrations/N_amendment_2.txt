AMENDMENT 2 TO N2
Written 2026-10-18 17:07 UTC, before any model of N2 was fitted or scored.

WHAT HAD BEEN COMPUTED
Only the pre-registered comparison on the development years: a regression on log(1 + the
number of headlines) alone, run on 16 October 2026 to check the pipeline end to end. It lost
25.06 bps a day after costs, of which 20 bps were the costs. No variant has been run, and
nothing on the holdout years.

THE CHANGE: COSTS
N2 buys and sells at the day's close and closes at the next close. An order on the close
(Alpaca's time in force `cls`, whole shares, sent by 15:50 New York time) fills at the
official closing price: no spread is paid, and an account of 30,000 USD moves no closing
auction. It pays the regulatory fees on sales, about 0.2 bps of the value sold (the SEC fee
in the project's cost model), and a short pays a borrow fee of a fraction of a basis point a
day for S&P 500 members.

On 18 October 2026 Alpaca's paper account accepted a whole-share market-on-close order for
SPY (status "accepted"; cancelled at once) and refused a fractional one ("fractional orders
must be DAY orders").

So N2's cost becomes 1 bp a round trip on each side, 2 bps a day, rounded up from the fees
and the borrow, in place of 10 bps a round trip on each side, 20 bps a day, which was a
placeholder. Positions are whole shares; their rounding is tracking error, not a cost, and
is not modelled.

The change follows from the measurement above, not from the count-only result. Everything
else in N2 is unchanged.
