Written 2026-10-15T18:17:06Z, before any of these was computed. Six ideas from the source
pool that our data can test. Each has a direction from its paper. Six at once: Bonferroni,
one-sided 2.5%/6, |t| >= 2.64. Data: 2016-01-05 to 2026-10-09 unless stated.

B1 Heston, Korajczyk, Sadka (JF 2010) at the index level: QQQ's return in a half hour of the
   day is positively related to the mean of its returns in the same half hour over the previous
   20 sessions. Pooled over the 13 half hours. Test: slope > 0.
B2 Harris (JFE 1986): QQQ's last 15 minutes (15:45 to the close) earn more than its average
   15 minutes of the day. Test: mean of (last 15 minutes' return minus 1/26 of the day's
   open-to-close return) > 0.
B3 Lou, Yan, Zhang (RFS 2013) Treasury auction cycle: TLT's close-to-close return over the
   5 sessions before a 10-year or 30-year Treasury auction is lower than over other 5-session
   windows; its return from the auction day's close over the next 5 sessions is higher. Test:
   (after minus before) > 0, over the auctions in 2016-2026. Auction dates from TreasuryDirect.
B4 Hirshleifer, Shumway (JF 2003): QQQ's open-to-close return is higher on mornings with less
   cloud in New York (Central Park / LaGuardia, 06:00-10:00 local). Test: slope on cloud cover
   < 0. Weather from a public archive (Meteostat or NOAA).
B5 Ang, Timmermann (2012) regimes: a two-state Gaussian hidden Markov model on QQQ's daily
   returns, fitted each year on the years before only; the noise-area rule's daily result is
   higher on days the model puts in its high-volatility state (momentum pays in turbulence,
   Gao et al. 2018). Test: difference > 0.
B6 Liu, Tsyvinski (RFS 2021); Shen, Urquhart, Wang (2022) crypto: Bitcoin's (BTC/USD, Alpaca)
   last-week return predicts next week's (time-series momentum), weekly, from Alpaca's first
   crypto bar to 2026-10-09. Test: slope > 0.
