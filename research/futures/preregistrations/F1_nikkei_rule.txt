Written 2026-10-17 13:18:46 UTC; approved by Efe before any Nikkei result was computed.

Does the QQQ rule, unchanged, earn on Nikkei 225 futures in the Tokyo session?

HYPOTHESIS
The noise-area intraday momentum rule (decision 0011, src/qfin_alphaguard/momentum.py), with
none of its settings changed, earns after costs on Nikkei 225 futures over the Tokyo Stock
Exchange's session. Mechanism: intraday momentum holds in index futures across developed
markets, Japan among them (Li, Sakkas and Urquhart 2022; Baltussen, Da, Lammers and Martens
2021, from the hedging of index options and leveraged ETFs, both large in Japan). The Tokyo
session does not overlap the US one, so a pass would add breadth to the QQQ rule.

DATA
- CME Nikkei 225 yen futures (NIY), 1-minute bars of the most traded contract each day
  (Databento GLBX.MDP3, NIY.v.0), 4 January 2016 to 9 October 2026, bought 17 October 2026
  (12.09 USD), data/raw/futures/. Nothing of it was looked at before this file but the bars'
  count and volume by hour and the contract each day.
- Sessions: the Tokyo Stock Exchange's trading days and hours from exchange_calendars
  (XTKS): 09:00 Tokyo time to the close (15:00 until 1 November 2024, 15:30 from
  5 November 2024). A minute without a trade carries the last price. The CME's minutes in the
  lunch break (11:30-12:30) are kept; no decision is taken in it.
- Rolls: a session whose contract differs from the previous session's is not traded, since
  its contract's previous close is not in the data (about four a year); its return is left
  out of the volatility windows.

THE RULE, AS FOR QQQ
- The noise band at each minute from the 14 sessions before: the mean of |price / open - 1|
  at that minute, around max and min of the open and the previous session's close.
- VWAP of the session so far, on CME volume.
- Decisions at :00 and :30 from 09:30 Tokyo time (none at 11:30 and 12:00): long above the
  band and VWAP, short below both, flat otherwise; each filled at the next minute's open.
- Size min(2, 2% / the standard deviation of the last 14 sessions' close-to-close returns).
- Flat at the open of the session's last minute.

COSTS
- Each fill pays one tick (5 index points) from the minute's open, plus 0.1 bps of fees: the
  CME's Tokyo-hours book is thin (7-20 contracts a minute). Reported also at half a tick.

IMPLEMENTATION CHECK, BEFORE ANY NIKKEI RESULT
- The same research code, run on QQQ's US sessions, must reproduce
  research/intraday_pilot/qqq_momentum.py's daily results: correlation at least 0.99, and
  the same trading days within 1%.
- 2016 is the implementation year: its Nikkei results may be looked at to find bugs, and
  are reported but not tested.

TEST, RUN ONCE
- The rule's daily return on Nikkei after one-tick costs, 3 January 2017 to 9 October 2026:
  one-sided t >= 2.0.
- Reported, not part of the pass: Sharpe ratio and each year; half-tick costs; the correlation
  of its daily returns with the QQQ rule's (the Tokyo session comes before the US one on the
  same date); and the Sharpe ratio of the two rules together at equal risk, each scaled by
  its volatility over the year before.
- If it passes: a decision record on adding Nikkei to the strategy, paper first (its UK route
  is Phase 3b's), and the FTSE 100 becomes worth its 55.82 USD. If it fails, it is recorded as
  failed.
