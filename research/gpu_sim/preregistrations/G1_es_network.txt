PRE-REGISTRATION G1
Written 2026-10-17 15:12:02 UTC; approved by Efe before any result of a network on real data was computed.

Does a small neural network, trained by evolution strategies on 16 ETFs, beat the QQQ rule?

HYPOTHESIS
A policy network that sees, at each of the rule's decisions, ten numbers known at the minute
before (where the price is against the noise band and VWAP, the moves since the open and in
the last half hour, the gap, the time, the volatility, the rule's own position and what it
holds) and chooses an exposure in [-2, 2], trained on the sessions of 16 ETFs from earlier
years only, earns more on QQQ than the rule at the same risk. What is new against step 5's
26 learned policies: sixteen ETFs to learn from, and evolution strategies on the GPU at a
scale the CPU did not allow (256 networks over 43,000 sessions in about 0.4 seconds).

DATA AND ENVIRONMENT
- research/gpu_sim/gym.py, as committed with this file: the 16 ETFs' stored minutes, every
  clean session (no split or missing session in what its observations read), the rule's
  decisions every 30 minutes from the open, fills at the next minute's open, everything
  closed at the session's last price, 0.4 bps of cost for each unit of exposure traded. The
  rule as a policy reproduces qqq_momentum.py exactly (test_gym.py).
- Training sessions for test year Y: all 16 ETFs, 2016 to Y - 1. Test sessions: QQQ in Y.

MODEL (Efe's; the protocol fixed here)
- research/gpu_sim/efe_net.py: one hidden layer of tanh units, the output 2 tanh(...).
  Efe chooses the hidden width (at most 64), and whether the network sees the rule's
  position (observation 8): a network blind to the rule gets 0 there, so that it can only
  find a strategy of its own. The rule stays the benchmark either way.
- Training: es.evolve, OpenAI-style evolution strategies, fitness the annual Sharpe ratio of
  the training sessions' returns after costs. Efe chooses the generations (at most 2,000),
  population (at most 256), sigma, learning rate and seed.
- At most 10 settings (each a choice of all the above) tried on development, each recorded
  in LEDGER.md with its result before the next is run.

SPLITS
- Development: test years 2018-2021 (es.walk_forward, es.DEVELOPMENT), as often as Efe likes
  within the 10 settings.
- Holdout: QQQ 2022 to 9 October 2026, each year trained from scratch on all years before
  it, run once (es.run_holdout) with the chosen settings committed in
  variants/G1_chosen.json; chosen by the highest development mean a day, a rule fixed here.

TEST
- The daily difference between the network on QQQ at the rule's risk (scaled by the ratio
  of the two's volatility on QQQ's training sessions) and the rule, over the holdout:
  one-sided t >= 2.0.
- Reported, not part of the pass: each year; the network's own Sharpe ratio; the rule's.
- If it passes, the network is frozen on a challenger card and judged forward in the shadow
  record (decision 0020); it trades only after a promotion there and Efe's decision. If it
  fails, it is recorded as failed.
