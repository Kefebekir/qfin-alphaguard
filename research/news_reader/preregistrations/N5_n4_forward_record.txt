PRE-REGISTRATION N5
Written 2026-10-21 14:23 UTC; approved by Efe at 14:24 UTC, before the record's first day.

Is N4 real? N4's chosen variant failed its holdout (+6.53 bps a day after costs, t +1.86),
and its result rested on 2026: +35.55 bps a day over 194 days, against +0.92 over 2022-2025.
Those years have been seen. Only days not yet seen can tell whether 2026's strength lasts. N5
runs N4's variant 2, frozen, forward, on each session from its first day, in the shadow record
(decision 0020), and judges it by the betting test, which may be read every evening.

THE CHALLENGER (frozen; a challenger card, challengers/news-n4-forward.yaml)
- The model: N4's variant 2 (efe_model.make(64, 10.0): 64 principal components and ridge,
  penalty 10; features base-last and log(1 + count); target scaled by volatility), fitted
  once on every member-day from 2016 to 9 October 2026, by Efe. Its weights, a file under
  data/models/, are pinned on the card by their SHA-256; it is never fitted again.
- The reader: PIT-4B-202412 (SHA-256 in the ledger), the latest point-in-time checkpoint,
  for every headline of the record, through pit.py, as N3 and N4 read 2025's and 2026's.
- The rules: N2's articles (tagging one or two stocks, members that day; Benzinga's symbols
  read as N2 reads them), window (16:00 on the session before to 15:30), and member-day
  features (the mean embedding and log(1 + count)); N4's variant 2's positions: long the
  members at or beyond the top tenth's edge, short those at or beyond the bottom's, equal
  weights, ties included (amendment 5), no trade with fewer than 20 members or edges that
  meet; costs of 1 bp a round trip on each side, 2 bps a day.

THE RECORD
- Headlines are captured as they stand at 15:31 New York time each session, from Alpaca's
  news, with the time of capture, so that a later edit cannot reach a decision. An article
  counts if it was known by 15:30 by N2's rule (from its edit when edited more than five
  minutes after it was created).
- After the next session's close the day is scored: the S&P 500 members that day, the
  captured headlines read and the frozen model's forecasts, the long-short's return from the
  day's close to the next, by the adjusted closes. A session whose capture failed has no
  score and is listed; it is not filled in afterwards.
- The first day is the first session after the card is committed and the capture has run
  for a full session. Nothing is changed during the record; a change makes a new card.

TEST
- The betting test of decision 0020 (evidence.BettingTest) on each day's score against no
  trade: scale 1, the rule's result 0 each day, clip 0.085 (the largest daily score of N4's
  variant 2 on its holdout, 8.48%, rounded up to 0.1%), alpha 0.0125: half of the platform's
  2.5%, Efe's choice. Promoted on the first evening its wealth reaches 80.
- The null hypothesis: every day, given what came before, the clipped score is expected to
  be at most 0. Then the chance of a promotion is at most 1.25%, however often it is read.
- A promotion is evidence, not a switch (decision 0020, 6): the strategy trades only by
  Efe's decision record, after he has seen the record, and within Guard's limits.
- Reviewed on 31 December 2028; Efe may retire the card earlier. Its share stays spent.

WHAT TO EXPECT (by resampling N4's holdout days, research only)
- If the days to come are like 2022-2026 as a whole, the test promotes within 2 years in
  about 1% of resampled records and within 5 in about 14%: an edge that weak cannot be
  shown in a useful time.
- If they are like 2026 alone, in about 41% within a year and 96% within 2, at a median of
  about 1.1 years.
- With no edge, in under 1% within 5 years.

REPORTED EACH EVENING, NOT PART OF THE TEST
- The days, the mean a day after costs and its t, the long and the short side apart, the
  wealth and its threshold, the days beyond the clip, each month.
- The observation from N4's 2026: the mean on earnings-season sessions (from the 15th of
  January, April, July and October to the last day of the month after) against the others,
  with the t of the difference.
